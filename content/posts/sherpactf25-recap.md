SherpaSec hosted SherpaCTF 2025, an innovative Battle Royale-style Capture the Flag (CTF) competition, held in conjunction with the Parallel Pulse conference in Malaysia. Breaking away from traditional CTF formats, this 6-hour intensive event challenged participants with a unique elimination-based competition structure, combining digital cybersecurity challenges with physical skill tests. The event aimed to push boundaries in competitive cybersecurity formats while fostering community engagement and identifying top talent in Malaysia's cybersecurity ecosystem.

We want to express our heartfelt thanks to every participant and supporter who made this event possible. Trying out a new CTF concept was a big step for us, and we were initially worried about how participants would react to it. But the smiles on your faces gave us our answer, and we truly appreciate every moment of excitement and enjoyment it brought to all of you! Plus, we hope you had fun with our non-CTF side quests too.

Special thanks to Parallel Pulse for having us at their excellent conference, and div0, HackingGroup, Signalens, Casaba, Altered Security and Verdesec for their incredible support throughout the journey. This competition wouldn’t have been possible without their help.
