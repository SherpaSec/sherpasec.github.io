Our twenty-second community session saw us reunite with Range Village for **AD CTF 2.0**, bringing together cybersecurity enthusiasts, students, and professionals for another evening of hands-on Active Directory challenges at Asia Pacific University.

Following the success of our first collaboration, AD CTF 2.0 continued to provide participants with practical experience in an Active Directory environment through a series of carefully curated challenges. Designed by Range Village, the CTF encouraged participants to strengthen their understanding of Active Directory security while exploring concepts such as enumeration, privilege escalation, lateral movement, and attack techniques in a realistic setting.

The session was made possible through our continued collaboration with Range Village, a community-led cyber range initiative dedicated to helping individuals build practical cybersecurity skills through curated labs and hands-on training.

Congratulations to all of the winners for their outstanding performance throughout the competition, and thank you to every participant who joined us and embraced the challenges. It was great to see the enthusiasm, teamwork, and determination displayed throughout the evening.

A special thank you to Range Village for partnering with us once again and for providing the environment that made this event possible. We look forward to bringing more hands-on learning opportunities to the community in the months ahead.

Until the next meetup!
