# Division Zero (DIV0)
<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; background: transparent; ">
    <img src="/images/partners/div0_white.png" alt="Div0">
</div></br>
The Singapore Cybersecurity Community, Div0, fosters a dynamic and secure cyberspace through its open, inclusive, and volunteer-powered ethos. Serving as a nexus for cybersecurity professionals, practitioners, and enthusiasts, Div0 offers a platform for connection, collaborative learning, and community engagement. Through its events, programs, and initiatives, Div0 facilitates knowledge sharing, fosters collaboration, and advocates for cybersecurity awareness among the public.

# The Hacking Group
<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; background: transparent;">
    <img src="/images/partners/THG_white.png" alt="TheHackingGroup">
</div></br>
The Hacking Group community is a non-profit, privately operated cybersecurity technology network founded by the seasoned security expert known as TNT. Since its official inception in June 2023, it has rapidly expanded across more than 70 regions—including Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, mainland China, and Hong Kong—organizing over 170 inclusive online and in-person events that foster collaborative engagement and shared learning. Anchored in a philosophy of technological exchange, Hacking Group builds bridges between emerging talents and veteran practitioners, advocating for a positive hacker culture, encouraging localized knowledge sharing, broader community participation, and the continuous evolution of cybersecurity practice.   
</br>
