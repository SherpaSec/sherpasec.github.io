Our special community CTF collaboration with Range Village wrapped up successfully, bringing together participants for an evening of hands-on challenges and practical learning.

Hosted at Asia Pacific University, the AD-focused CTF ran for three hours and gave participants the chance to improve their skills in areas such as Active Directory testing, lateral movement, and defence evasion. The challenges were designed in partnership with Range Village, a community-led initiative dedicated to creating curated labs and practical exercises for cybersecurity enthusiasts.

Congratulations to all the winners who took home some well-earned swag, and a big thank-you to every participant who joined in and made the session lively and competitive. We hope everyone left with new insights into AD security and fresh perspectives on real-world attack techniques.

Thank you once again to Range Village for collaborating with us, and to the community for showing up and making this CTF a success. Until the next one!
